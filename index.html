NoorBeads Privacy Policy

Simple, honest and short: your data stays on your device.

App: NoorBeads Package: com.majidsadiaapp.noorbeads Effective date: October 13, 2026

Summary

NoorBeads does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal information. The app works fully offline, needs no account and asks for no permissions.

1.About this policy

This policy explains how the NoorBeads Android app (the "App"), provided by [Developer Name] ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

2.Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers or any account details
  • location data
  • contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • usage analytics or crash reports

3.Information stored on your device

To work properly, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your phone. This data never leaves your device through the App.

DataPurpose
Current count and completed roundsKeeps your place when you close the App
Selected target (33, 99 or 100)Remembers your preference
Selected bead styleRemembers your preference
Light or dark mode choiceRemembers your preference
Daily totalsShows your daily history inside the App

4.Permissions

NoorBeads does not request any Android permissions. It does not use the internet, location, storage access, camera, microphone, or vibration.

5.Advertising, analytics and third parties

The App contains no advertisements, no analytics tools and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not share information with any third party because we do not receive any.

6.Device backup

Android may include app data in your device backup if you have turned on backup in your Google account settings. Any such backup is created and controlled by Google and your device settings. We cannot access it. Please refer to Google's own privacy information for details about backups.

7.Your control and deletion

Because all data is on your device, you are in full control:

  • Use Reset in the App to set your current counter back to zero.
  • Open the daily history and choose Clear to delete saved daily totals.
  • Clear the App's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the App, to remove all of its data.

8.Children's privacy

The App is not directed at young children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, since the App collects no personal data.

9.Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage area on your device, which is protected by the Android operating system. No data is transmitted, so there is nothing to intercept in transit.

10.Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and revise the effective date above. If a future version of the App ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
